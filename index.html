PlacementPath Explainability Framework

Transparent mathematical model for student readiness scoring

Unlike opaque black-box AI systems, PlacementPath operates on transparent, explainable, rule-based algorithms. Every recommendation, gap score, and readiness percentage can be traced directly to clear mathematical formulas:

1. Overall Placement Readiness Index (0 to 100)

Readiness = (Technical × 0.35) + (DSA × 0.30) + (Aptitude × 0.20) + (Communication × 0.15)

Derived from hiring round weightings in campus placement drives. Technical and DSA rounds filter over 70% of candidates during initial online tests (OA) and technical rounds.

2. Calibrated Skill Rating

Proficiency = (Assessment Score × 0.70) + (Self-Assessed Baseline × 0.30)

When you complete an assessment, objective evaluation receives 70% weight, correcting any over- or under-confidence in self-reporting.

3. Skill Gap & Priority Matrix

Gap = max(0, Role Target Benchmark - Current Proficiency)
  • Critical Gap (≥ 20 pts): Immediate placement bottleneck. Must be addressed before scheduling campus interviews.
  • Moderate Gap (10–19 pts): Important competency requiring 4–6 hours of targeted practice.
  • Target Met (≤ 9 pts): Candidate meets or exceeds the industry baseline for entry-level hiring.
